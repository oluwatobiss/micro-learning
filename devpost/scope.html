MicroLearning Studio

A creator-first web application turning long-form educational expertise into polished, source-grounded, interactive micro-learning lessons.

The Unique Kernel
Instead of generic AI summarizers or disconnected flashcard decks, the system adaptively extracts the source's natural structure to produce a coherent, unified narrative card deck with source-grounded comprehension checks and instant explanations.

Creators retain full editorial authority before publishing, while busy readers get an anti-doomscroll, high-retention learning experience in minutes with zero friction.

The Complete Value Loop & Architecture
Who It's For

Knowledge Creators: Authors, educators, newsletter writers, and technical specialists with published books, articles, or chapters who want to give readers high-retention interactive access without spending days rebuilding social carousels or quiz decks manually.

Inspiration & Ethos

Rooted in direct personal author experience. Focused on native web standards, clean CSS styling, distraction-free reading, anti-doomscroll pacing, and zero unnecessary UI library bloat.

What "Done" Looks Like (60-Second Demo)
1. Input / Source Selection
Creator pastes article text or clicks "Load Sample Article".
2. Adaptive Generation
AI plans a coherent lesson, extracting narrative cards and source-grounded checks adaptively sized to source density.
3. Studio Review & Polish
Creator reviews cards, edits text, tweaks questions/explanations, and clicks "Preview / Publish".
4. Friction-Free Reader Journey
Standalone reader view loads immediately. Reader steps through the card sequence, answers an interactive check, sees immediate feedback and explanation, and reaches completion with a source link.
The POC Boundary
  • Source Input: Article paste box + quick "Load Sample Article" button.
  • Adaptive Generation: Dynamically sizes card count (e.g. 4–10 cards) and places checks based on source concepts rather than rigid templates.
  • Source-Grounded Checks: Interactive questions with verified answers and educational explanations.
  • Creator Studio: Visual card deck editor (edit text, reorder, adjust questions).
  • Reader Experience: Distraction-free card deck player with visible Next/Prev/Progress controls, feedback modal, and completion screen.
  • Architecture: Lean, type-safe implementation with native CSS and zero UI framework bloat.
  • Creator Authentication: Multi-tenant login, profiles, and workspace security.
  • Cloud Persistence: Database storage and hosted public custom URLs.
  • Learning Analytics: Drop-off graphs, card read times, and comprehension accuracy rates.
  • Social Carousel Export: Direct export of cards to LinkedIn/Twitter image carousels (PNG/PDF).
  • Generic Summarizers: Discarded because bulleted summaries fail to build a learning narrative.
  • Disconnected Flashcard Decks: Discarded because isolated flashcards lack structural context.
  • Infinite Feeds & Gamified Streaks: Discarded because doomscrolling undermines deep comprehension and retention.
